# Automate Your Square Payments with n8n: Introducing n8n-nodes-square

We're excited to announce the release of n8n-nodes-square, a powerful community node that brings Square's comprehensive payment and business management capabilities directly into your n8n workflows!

## The Problem We're Solving

Square has become an essential platform for businesses managing payments, inventory, and customer relationships. However, many businesses struggle to connect Square with their other tools and systems. Whether you need to sync customer data to your CRM, generate custom reports from payment data, or automate inventory updates across platforms, doing this manually is time-consuming and error-prone.

That's where n8n-nodes-square comes in.

## Key Features

Our Square node provides comprehensive access to Square's most important APIs, allowing you to automate:

- **Payments**: Process transactions, track payment history, and trigger workflows based on payment events
- **Orders**: Manage orders, update statuses, and integrate with fulfillment systems
- **Customers**: Sync customer data, segment audiences, and personalize communications
- **Catalog**: Update product listings, manage pricing, and keep your catalog synchronized across platforms
- **Inventory**: Track stock levels, automate reorder processes, and prevent overselling
- **Invoices**: Create, send, and track invoices automatically

## Real-World Use Cases

Imagine automatically adding new Square customers to your email marketing platform, syncing inventory levels with your e-commerce store, or sending custom thank-you messages after purchases. With n8n-nodes-square, these workflows become simple to build and maintain.

## Getting Started

Installing the node is straightforward. In your n8n instance, run:


npm install n8n-nodes-square


After restarting n8n, you'll find the Square node available in your node palette, ready to connect with your Square account.
